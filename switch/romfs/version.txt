BO1 Zombies — Nintendo Switch homebrew port

The game data is NOT inside this NRO. Stage it on the SD card at:
    sdmc:/switch/bo1zm/
(the converted AArch64 IWffs zones plus the Steam install's main/*.iwd).

This romfs is embedded so that romfsInit() succeeds: a homebrew NRO with no
romfs makes Ryujinx throw on OpenDataStorageByCurrentProcess. The build id is
logged at start-up (see boot.log on the SD card), per switch-homebrew.md.
